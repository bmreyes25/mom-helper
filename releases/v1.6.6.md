MOM Helper v1.6.6 is for supported **Calculus I** questions on MyOpenMath. **Physics and Calculus II are not supported yet; both are planned for later releases.**

### What's new

- Recognizes piecewise-function definitions in MyOpenMath's MathJax array form even when they omit “if.”
- Solves and maps all three fields in the newest missed capture: `f(-1)=-5`, `f(0)=8`, `f(2)=26`.
- Adds the latest 14-question capture to local regression testing. All 215 usable stored captured states solve deterministically; four historical incomplete captures remain excluded.
- Publishes the project page, current scope, and installation instructions.

The extension shows steps and can fill supported answer fields, but it **never submits** a question. Optional AI fallback is disabled by default. Offline test results do not guarantee every future question or live widget behavior.

To install, download and unzip the attached file, open `chrome://extensions`, enable Developer mode, choose **Load unpacked**, and select the extracted `myopenmath-helper-v1.6.6` folder.
